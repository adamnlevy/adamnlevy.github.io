## The project

Completed a custom order while working with the Exploring Opportunities Program (EOP) at Richmond Green Secondary School.

## The process

Prepared a live-edge wood slab by planing it to create a smooth surface. Combined resin with dyes to achieve the desired colours, then poured the mixture and allowed it to cure. Sanded and polished the finished piece, and attached the legs.

## Skills developed

Built practical skills in mixing resin with dyes, applying even heat to support a clean pour with fewer air bubbles, and sanding and polishing for a smooth finish.
