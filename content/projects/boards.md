## The Idea

Inspired by my dad’s work as a chef, I set out to create boards for use in and around the kitchen. Working with a variety of woods, I made cutting boards, charcuterie boards, and decorative boards.

## The Process

I shape individual or multiple thin wood slabs into functional boards and distinctive forms. I glue the pieces together, plane them flat, then route and sand them as needed. Each board is finished with selected oils, waxes, or rubs.

## The Result

Through this project, I developed hands-on experience using a planer, miter saw, palm sander, router table, drill press, and table saw. I also learned to apply a range of wood finishes and adhesives, and to work with live-edge pieces while preserving their natural features.
