## The prompt

I wanted a place for the small objects that migrate around my desk: a pen, a memory card, a roll of tape, and the inevitable handful of screws. The constraint was simple: it had to be useful without taking over the desk.

## Design and process

The organizer is a family of three stackable modules. I modeled each part in CAD with the same footprint and printed them in PETG so they could survive a little daily abuse. A shallow chamfer on every edge keeps the parts friendly to handle and gives the shadows somewhere to land.

The first prototype was too deep and collected dust. Reducing the depth by 18 mm made a surprising difference. The final version prints without supports and takes roughly three hours per module.

## Result

It is a small object, but it earns its footprint. The modular approach also means the system can grow when the desk does—no new design required.
