## Why make a kit?

Good work often starts before the first cut. This little kit collects the references I reach for most: a scale, a set of repeatable offsets, and a notebook that stays open on the bench.

## What changed

The first version was a beautiful nuisance. It had too many markings and no good place for a pencil. I rebuilt it around three actions—measure, mark, record—and kept the materials honest: birch ply, a scrap of leather, and a brass fastener.

The result is not a universal tool. It is a personal prompt to slow down long enough to make the next decision legible.

> A good jig is a frozen decision. A good notebook keeps that decision available.
